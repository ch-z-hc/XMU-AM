##  质点运动方程

$$
s = s(t)
$$

## 速度

$$
\vec{v} = |v| \vec{e_t} = \frac{ds}{dt} \vec{e_t}
$$

## 加速度

$$
\vec a = \frac{d}{dt} \vec v = \frac{d |v|}{dt} \vec {e_t} + \frac{d \vec {e_t}}{dt} |v|
$$

将 $\vec {e_t}$ 和 $\vec {e_n}$ 画在直角坐标系中，有：
$$
\vec {e_t} = \cos \theta \vec i + \sin \theta \vec j \\
\vec {e_n} = -\sin \theta \vec i + \cos \theta \vec j
$$
带入上式，有：
$$
\vec a = \frac{d |v|}{dt} \vec {e_t} + \frac{d \theta}{dt} \vec {e_n} |v| \\
= \frac{d |v|}{dt} \vec {e_t} + \frac{d \theta}{ds} \vec {e_n} |v|^2 \\
= \frac{d |v|}{dt} \vec {e_t} + \frac{1}{\rho} |v|^2 \vec {e_n}
$$

## 力

$$
\vec F = \frac{d}{dt} \vec p = \frac{d}{dt} (m\vec v) = \frac{d}{dt} m \cdot \vec v + \frac{d}{dt} \vec v \cdot m \\
= \frac{d}{dt} m \cdot \vec v + \left( \frac{d |v|}{dt} \vec {e_t} + \frac{1}{\rho} |v|^2 \vec {e_n} \right)\cdot m
$$



# 极坐标

![image-20241021200059174](C:\Users\chzhc\AppData\Roaming\Typora\typora-user-images\image-20241021200059174.png)

##  质点运动方程

$$
r = r(t) \\
\theta  = \theta(t)
$$

## 位移

$$
\vec r = |r| \vec {e_r}
$$

## 速度

对位移 $\vec r$ 求导：
$$
\vec v = \frac{d}{dt} \vec r = \frac{d}{dt} (|r|\vec {e_r}) = \frac{d}{dt} |r| \cdot \vec {e_r} + \frac{d}{dt} \vec {e_r} \cdot |r|
$$
将 $\vec {e_r}$ 和 $\vec {e_\theta}$ 画在直角坐标系中，有：
$$
\vec {e_r} = \cos \theta \vec i + \sin \theta \vec j \\
\vec {e_\theta} = -\sin \theta \vec i + \cos \theta \vec j
$$
带入上式，有：
$$
\frac{d}{dt} \vec r =  \frac{d}{dt} |r| \cdot \vec {e_r} + \frac{d\theta}{d t}\vec {e_\theta} \cdot |r|
$$

## 加速度

$$
\vec a = \frac{d}{dt} \vec v = \frac{d}{dt} \left( \frac{d}{dt} |r| \cdot \vec {e_r} + \frac{d\theta}{d t}\vec {e_\theta} \cdot |r|\right) \\
= \frac{d}{dt} \frac{d}{dt} |r| \cdot \vec {e_r} + \frac{d}{dt} |r|  \frac{d}{dt} \vec {e_r} + 

\left(\frac{d}{dt} \frac{d\theta}{d t} \right)\vec {e_\theta} \cdot |r| + 
\frac{d\theta}{d t} \left(\frac{d}{dt} \vec {e_\theta} \right) \cdot |r| +

\frac{d\theta}{d t}\vec {e_\theta} \cdot \left(\frac{d}{dt}|r|\right) \\

=\vec {e_r} \left(\ddot {|r|} - |r|\dot{\theta}^2 \right) + 
\vec{e_\theta} \left(2\dot{|r|}\dot{\theta} + \ddot \theta ^2|r| \right)
$$

# 功

元功：
$$
dA = \vec F \cdot d \vec r
$$
功率：
$$
P = \frac{dA}{dt} = \vec F \cdot \vec v
$$

## 直角坐标系

$$
\vec F = F_x \vec i + F_y \vec j + F_z \vec k \\
d \vec r = dx \vec i + dy \vec j + dz \vec k \\
\vec v = v_x \vec i + v_y \vec j  +  v_z \vec k \\
dA = F_x dx +  F_y dy + F_z dz \\
P = F_x v_x + F_y v_y + F_z v_z
$$

## 自然坐标系

![image-20241022210702079](C:\Users\chzhc\AppData\Roaming\Typora\typora-user-images\image-20241022210702079.png)
$$
\vec F = F_t \vec{e_t} + F_n \vec{e_n} \\
d \vec r = d s \vec {e_t} \\
\vec v = v \vec {e_t} \\
dA = F_t ds \\
P = F_t v
$$

## 极坐标系

![image-20241022210721728](C:\Users\chzhc\AppData\Roaming\Typora\typora-user-images\image-20241022210721728.png)
$$
\vec F = F_r \vec{e_r} + F_\theta \vec{e_\theta} \\
d \vec r = dr \cdot \vec {e_r} + d\theta\vec {e_\theta} \cdot r \\
\vec v =  \frac{d r}{dt} \cdot \vec {e_r} + \frac{d\theta}{d t}\vec {e_\theta} \cdot r \\
dA = F_r dr + F_\theta  rd \theta \\
P = F_r \frac{dr}{dt} + F_\theta r \frac{d\theta}{dt}
$$

# 动能定理

$$
A = \int _a^b \vec F \cdot d\vec r = \int _a^b \frac{d(m \vec v)}{dt} \cdot d\vec r \\
= \int _a^b m  \vec v d\vec v = \left ( \frac{1}{2} m\vec v^2\right) \Bigg |_a^b \\
= \frac{1}{2}mv_b^2 - \frac{1}{2}mv_a^2
$$
